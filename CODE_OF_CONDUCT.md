# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We work as a respectful, inclusive, and technically honest team. Welcome questions, explain decisions, give actionable feedback, and assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and conduct that prevents others from participating safely.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers or security contact. Do not include sensitive personal data in public issues. Reports will be handled confidentially where possible and retaliation is not accepted.
