# PHASE 35 — LONG-TERM GOVERNANCE, PLATFORM SUSTAINABILITY & INSTITUTIONAL STEWARDSHIP

## Objective

Establish the governance structures, institutional stewardship, sustainability mechanisms, and long-term operational model required to ensure that StatGate remains relevant, secure, financially sustainable, technologically current, and community-driven for decades.

This phase defines how the platform will evolve after its initial launch while preserving its mission, quality, and strategic direction.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become a long-lived institution rather than a short-term software project.

Its success shall be measured not only by technology, but by the trust it earns, the communities it serves, and the evidence it enables.
