# PHASE 20 — DEVOPS, CI/CD, DEPLOYMENT, OBSERVABILITY & PRODUCTION OPERATIONS

## Objective

Develop the complete production engineering platform responsible for building, testing, deploying, monitoring, scaling, maintaining, and operating StatGate across development, testing, staging, production, and sovereign cloud environments.

This phase establishes the operational foundation required for high availability, resilience, continuous delivery, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Vision

Every deployment shall be automated.

Every release shall be reproducible.

Every service shall be observable.

Every failure shall be detectable.

Every environment shall remain consistent.

StatGate shall support deployments ranging from a single institution to nationwide implementations.
