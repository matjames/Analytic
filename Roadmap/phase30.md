# PHASE 30 — GLOBAL FEDERATION, DIGITAL DIPLOMACY & CROSS-BORDER COLLABORATION

## Objective

Develop the Global Federation Framework enabling sovereign organizations, governments, regional blocs, universities, NGOs, research institutions, and international agencies to collaborate securely while maintaining complete ownership of their respective data.

StatGate shall support regional, continental, and global evidence ecosystems through interoperable federation, allowing independent deployments to exchange selected data, knowledge, indicators, metadata, and analytical outputs without centralizing sensitive information.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become Africa's first globally federated Evidence Intelligence Platform.

Each participating organization shall retain full control over its infrastructure while collaborating through trusted, policy-driven federation.

The platform shall support national sovereignty, regional cooperation, and international evidence exchange simultaneously.
