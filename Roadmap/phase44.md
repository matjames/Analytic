# PHASE 44 — ENTERPRISE GEOSPATIAL INTELLIGENCE, REMOTE SENSING & NATIONAL SPATIAL DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive geospatial intelligence platform that integrates GIS, remote sensing, satellite imagery, drone technologies, spatial analytics, environmental intelligence, and national spatial data infrastructure into the StatGate ecosystem.

The platform shall support advanced mapping, spatial statistics, environmental monitoring, disaster response, infrastructure planning, and evidence-based geographic decision-making.

---

# Vision

Every dataset shall have geographic context.

Every decision shall be spatially informed.

Maps shall become analytical tools rather than static visualizations.

Geospatial intelligence shall become a core component of evidence generation.
