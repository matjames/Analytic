# PHASE 28 — BLOCKCHAIN, DIGITAL IDENTITY & TRUST SERVICES

## Objective

Develop the trust layer for StatGate using blockchain-inspired integrity mechanisms, verifiable credentials, decentralized identity standards where appropriate, digital signatures, and immutable audit services.

The objective is to strengthen authenticity, integrity, and trust while supporting secure verification of research outputs, statistical publications, certificates, approvals, and institutional records.

The architecture shall remain modular so organizations can choose whether to enable distributed ledger capabilities based on their governance and regulatory requirements.

# Vision

Evidence shall be trusted.

Every certificate shall be verifiable.

Every approval shall be traceable.

Every publication shall preserve its integrity throughout its lifecycle.
