# PHASE 49 — ENTERPRISE OPERATIONS CENTERS, PLATFORM OBSERVABILITY & NATIONAL COMMAND ECOSYSTEM

## Objective

Establish a comprehensive Enterprise Operations Center (EOC) ecosystem that provides centralized monitoring, management, coordination, security, reliability, incident response, business continuity, and operational intelligence across every StatGate deployment.

This phase transforms StatGate into a continuously monitored, self-observing, highly resilient platform capable of supporting governments, ministries, universities, NGOs, development partners, healthcare systems, and enterprise organizations through mission-critical operations.

The Operations Center shall function as the operational nerve center of the entire StatGate ecosystem.
