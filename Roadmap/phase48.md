# PHASE 48 — ENTERPRISE BUSINESS PROCESS MANAGEMENT, WORKFLOW AUTOMATION & DIGITAL OPERATIONS

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Business Process Management (BPM) platform that enables organizations to model, execute, automate, monitor, optimize, and continuously improve business processes across every StatGate module.

The platform shall provide standards-based workflow orchestration, intelligent automation, process mining, case management, and digital operations capabilities that support efficient, transparent, and auditable organizational processes.
