# PHASE 46 — LABORATORY INFORMATION MANAGEMENT, SCIENTIFIC INSTRUMENT INTEGRATION & DIGITAL LABORATORIES

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) and Scientific Instrument Integration Platform that enables laboratories, research institutions, hospitals, universities, public health agencies, environmental laboratories, agricultural laboratories, and industrial laboratories to manage the complete laboratory lifecycle within StatGate.

The platform shall support laboratory workflows from sample registration to result reporting while integrating directly with scientific instruments, quality management systems, research projects, biobanks, surveillance systems, and artificial intelligence.

Every laboratory process shall be digitally managed.
