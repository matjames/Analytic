# PHASE 25 — SOVEREIGN CLOUD, MULTI-CLOUD & NATIONAL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

## Objective

Develop the sovereign cloud architecture that enables StatGate to operate securely across government data centers, institutional infrastructure, commercial cloud providers, and hybrid environments while preserving national data sovereignty.

The platform shall support single-organization deployments, national deployments, regional deployments, and international federated deployments.

---

# Vision

Organizations shall own their infrastructure.

Governments shall own their data.

Deployments shall be portable across any infrastructure.

No organization shall be locked into a single cloud provider.
