# PHASE 26 — COMMERCIAL ECOSYSTEM, GLOBAL EXPANSION & DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION PLATFORM

## Objective

Transform StatGate into a globally deployable platform capable of supporting commercial operations, international partnerships, software licensing, enterprise consulting, managed services, and digital transformation initiatives.

This phase establishes the business ecosystem required for long-term sustainability and worldwide adoption.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become the leading African-developed Evidence Intelligence Platform serving governments, NGOs, universities, multilateral organizations, and enterprises across the world.

The platform shall support both open collaboration and commercial service delivery while maintaining its commitment to evidence, transparency, and data sovereignty.
