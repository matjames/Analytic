# PHASE 16 — ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION PLATFORM, APIs & INTEROPERABILITY

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Enterprise Integration Platform (EIP) that enables StatGate to seamlessly exchange data and services with internal systems, government platforms, development partners, cloud providers, AI services, and third-party applications.

This phase establishes StatGate as an interoperable digital platform capable of acting as both a data producer and data consumer while maintaining security, governance, and auditability.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become an integration hub for evidence ecosystems.

Organizations should be able to connect StatGate with existing information systems without modifying their core applications.

Every integration shall be secure, versioned, monitored, and documented.
