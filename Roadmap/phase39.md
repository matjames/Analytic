# PHASE 39 — ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE GRAPH, SEMANTIC INTELLIGENCE & KNOWLEDGE DISCOVERY

## Objective

Develop the Enterprise Knowledge Graph that connects every entity, dataset, publication, indicator, project, organization, policy, researcher, variable, workflow, and evidence artifact within StatGate into a unified semantic ecosystem.

This phase establishes the semantic foundation that enables intelligent search, advanced reasoning, explainable AI, knowledge discovery, and evidence relationship mapping.

---

# Vision

Knowledge shall not exist in isolated systems.

Every entity shall be connected.

Every relationship shall be discoverable.

Every recommendation shall be explainable.

Artificial Intelligence shall reason using structured knowledge rather than isolated datasets.
