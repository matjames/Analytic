# PHASE 17 — PUBLIC PORTALS, OPEN DATA, PUBLICATIONS & KNOWLEDGE DISSEMINATION

## Objective

Develop the complete public-facing ecosystem of StatGate, enabling organizations to disseminate official statistics, research outputs, dashboards, publications, open datasets, policies, media, and institutional knowledge to citizens, researchers, governments, development partners, and the global community.

This phase establishes StatGate as a trusted platform for evidence dissemination and public engagement.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become the digital gateway through which organizations publish trustworthy evidence and engage with the public.

Information shall be discoverable, accessible, reusable, and presented through modern, interactive experiences.
