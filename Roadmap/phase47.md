# PHASE 47 — ENTERPRISE SEARCH, DISCOVERY, UNIVERSAL INDEXING & INTELLIGENT INFORMATION RETRIEVAL

## Objective

Develop a unified Enterprise Search Platform capable of indexing, discovering, ranking, and retrieving every digital asset managed within StatGate through intelligent semantic search, natural language querying, AI-assisted discovery, and federated indexing.

The platform shall provide a single search experience across all modules, enabling users to rapidly locate structured and unstructured information regardless of where it is stored.

---

# Vision

Nothing within StatGate shall remain hidden.

Every authorized user shall discover relevant information instantly.

Search shall understand meaning, context, relationships, and user intent.

Knowledge shall become immediately accessible.
