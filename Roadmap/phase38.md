# PHASE 38 — SCIENTIFIC COMPUTING, STATISTICAL COMPUTING & HIGH-PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

## Objective

Develop the Scientific Computing Platform that transforms StatGate into a comprehensive computational environment for statistics, epidemiology, economics, public health, artificial intelligence, simulation, optimization, and scientific research.

The platform shall provide integrated computational workspaces supporting reproducible science, collaborative analysis, large-scale computation, and advanced statistical modeling.

---

# Vision

Every researcher shall have access to enterprise-grade scientific computing.

Every statistical analysis shall be reproducible.

Every model shall be version-controlled.

Every experiment shall be documented.

Every computational result shall be traceable.
