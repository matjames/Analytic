# PHASE 33 — ENTERPRISE EXCELLENCE, QUALITY MANAGEMENT & ORGANIZATIONAL PERFORMANCE

## Objective

Establish the Enterprise Excellence Framework that embeds quality, continuous improvement, organizational performance, operational maturity, and institutional learning across every component of StatGate.

This phase ensures that StatGate is not only technically advanced but also operationally excellent, measurable, and continuously improving.

The framework shall align software engineering, project delivery, organizational management, and evidence generation with internationally recognized quality standards.
