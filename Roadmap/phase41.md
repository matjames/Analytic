# PHASE 41 — NATIONAL OPEN DATA PLATFORM, PUBLIC EVIDENCE PORTAL & TRANSPARENCY ECOSYSTEM

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Open Data and Public Evidence Platform that enables governments, organizations, researchers, development partners, civil society, and citizens to discover, access, analyze, visualize, and reuse publicly available datasets, indicators, publications, and evidence products.

The platform shall strengthen transparency, accountability, innovation, and public participation while protecting confidential and restricted information through robust governance and access controls.

---

# Vision

Public evidence shall be accessible.

Open data shall encourage innovation.

Transparency shall strengthen trust.

Organizations shall publish high-quality, machine-readable datasets for responsible public use.
