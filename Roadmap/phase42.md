# PHASE 42 — LOW-CODE, NO-CODE DEVELOPMENT PLATFORM & CITIZEN APPLICATION BUILDER

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Low-Code and No-Code Development Platform that enables technical and non-technical users to design, build, deploy, extend, and maintain enterprise applications within StatGate without extensive programming.

The platform shall empower organizations to rapidly develop forms, workflows, dashboards, mobile applications, surveys, reports, integrations, automations, plugins, and AI-powered solutions while maintaining enterprise governance, security, and quality standards.

---

# Vision

Application development shall become accessible to everyone.

Organizations shall rapidly build digital solutions without sacrificing governance, scalability, or security.

Citizen developers shall complement professional software engineers through governed, reusable, and standardized application development.
