# PHASE 45 — ENTERPRISE FINANCE, GRANTS, ERP & RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) ecosystem that enables organizations to manage financial resources, grants, procurement, assets, inventories, human resources, contracts, and operational expenditures within the StatGate platform.

The platform shall provide complete financial accountability while integrating directly with projects, research, monitoring and evaluation, procurement, and organizational governance.

---

# Vision

Every financial transaction shall be accountable.

Every grant shall be traceable.

Every asset shall be managed throughout its lifecycle.

Financial intelligence shall support evidence-based organizational management.
