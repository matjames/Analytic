# PHASE 37 — ENTERPRISE DATA ENGINEERING, DATAOPS & MODERN DATA PLATFORM

## Objective

Design and implement the complete Enterprise Data Engineering Platform that powers every component of StatGate through reliable, scalable, observable, and intelligent data pipelines.

This phase establishes StatGate as a modern Data Intelligence Platform capable of ingesting, processing, transforming, validating, storing, governing, cataloging, monitoring, and serving structured, semi-structured, streaming, geospatial, multimedia, and scientific datasets.

The platform shall adopt modern DataOps principles to automate data lifecycle management, improve data quality, and accelerate analytics and artificial intelligence.

---

# Vision

Data shall become a governed enterprise asset.

Every dataset shall have a defined owner, lineage, quality score, metadata, lifecycle, and governance policy.

Every pipeline shall be observable.

Every transformation shall be reproducible.

Every decision shall be traceable back to trusted data.
