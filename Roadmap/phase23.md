# PHASE 23 — NATIONAL STATISTICAL SYSTEM, FEDERATION & INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATION

## Objective

Develop the National Statistical System (NSS) framework enabling multiple organizations, ministries, agencies, districts, research institutions, development partners, and international organizations to collaborate within a federated evidence ecosystem.

This phase positions StatGate as the backbone for national, regional, and cross-border statistical coordination.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall support the complete National Statistical System while allowing each institution to maintain ownership of its data through secure federation.

The platform shall facilitate collaboration between government, academia, NGOs, development partners, and international organizations without compromising data sovereignty.
