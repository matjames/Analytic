# PHASE 15 — DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT, RECORDS MANAGEMENT & DIGITAL ARCHIVES

## Objective

Develop the Enterprise Document and Records Management ecosystem that enables organizations to securely create, organize, version, approve, archive, search, preserve, and share institutional knowledge.

Every document produced within StatGate shall become part of the organization's permanent knowledge repository while complying with governance, legal, and records management standards.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall become the institutional memory of organizations.

No document should ever be lost.

Every version shall be preserved.

Every approval shall be traceable.

Every policy, report, publication, contract, protocol, proposal, dataset, and meeting record shall have a governed lifecycle.
