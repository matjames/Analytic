# PHASE 18 — MARKETPLACE, PLUGIN ECOSYSTEM & EXTENSION FRAMEWORK

## Objective

Develop the StatGate Marketplace and Extension Framework that enables organizations, developers, universities, government agencies, NGOs, and commercial partners to extend StatGate without modifying the core platform.

The Marketplace shall transform StatGate into a modular ecosystem where capabilities can be installed, upgraded, shared, licensed, or developed independently.

---

# Vision

StatGate shall function as an Enterprise Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS).

Every organization shall be able to customize its implementation through officially supported plugins, applications, themes, templates, workflows, AI models, dashboards, statistical packages, GIS tools, and integrations.

The core platform shall remain lightweight while extensions deliver domain-specific functionality.
