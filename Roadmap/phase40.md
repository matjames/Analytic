# PHASE 40 — NATIONAL DIGITAL LIBRARY, KNOWLEDGE REPOSITORY & RESEARCH PRESERVATION

## Objective

Develop a comprehensive National Digital Library and Enterprise Knowledge Repository that preserves, organizes, indexes, curates, and disseminates scientific knowledge, official publications, institutional memory, research outputs, statistical reports, multimedia resources, and historical records.

The platform shall ensure long-term digital preservation, facilitate knowledge sharing, support open science principles where applicable, and provide trusted access to institutional knowledge while respecting copyright, licensing, and access controls.

This phase establishes StatGate as the permanent knowledge memory of organizations, governments, universities, research institutions, NGOs, and development partners.

---

# Vision

Knowledge shall never be lost.

Every publication shall remain discoverable.

Every research output shall be preserved.

Every institutional lesson shall contribute to future knowledge.

The platform shall become the trusted repository of evidence across generations.
