# PHASE 27 — INTERNET OF THINGS (IoT), SMART DEVICES & REAL-TIME SENSOR INTELLIGENCE

## Objective

Develop the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem enabling StatGate to ingest, manage, analyze, and visualize data from connected devices, sensors, drones, laboratory equipment, environmental monitoring stations, medical devices, smart infrastructure, and industrial systems.

This phase transforms StatGate into a real-time evidence platform where physical infrastructure continuously contributes data for analytics and decision-making.

---

# Vision

Every sensor shall become a data source.

Every connected device shall contribute to evidence generation.

Real-time intelligence shall support governments, researchers, NGOs, healthcare systems, agriculture, transportation, manufacturing, and environmental monitoring.
