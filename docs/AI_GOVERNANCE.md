# StatGate Sovereign AI Governance & Decision Architecture

**Document Version:** 1.0 (Phase y Convergence)  
**Status:** Authoritative AI Governance Directive  
**Scope:** Institutional AI Agents, Anomaly Investigation, and Executive Recommendations  
**Directives:** Section 17 AI Governance Standard

---

## 1. Primary AI Governance Directive

Institutional decisions directly impact public policy, healthcare allocation, capital investments, and regulatory compliance.

Therefore, StatGate strictly enforces:

```text
    EVIDENCE (Datasets, Submissions, Files, Metrics)
          ↓
     CONTEXT (Universal Object Graph & History)
          ↓
    ANALYSIS (Statistical & Pattern Inference)
          ↓
 RECOMMENDATION (Structured Guidance with Confidence Score)
          ↓
  HUMAN DECISION (Authenticated Officer Approval & Signature)
```

**Under no circumstances shall AI autonomously commit institutional actions, release funding, publish official research, or alter legal records.**

---

## 2. Distinguishability & Evidence Traceability

Every output generated by the AI subsystem must clearly identify:
1. **Provenance:** Underlying datasets, surveys, and object URNs used as input.
2. **Confidence Metric:** Statistical certainty score (0.0 to 1.0).
3. **Classification:** Labeled explicitly as `AI_RECOMMENDATION`, clearly distinct from `VERIFIED_FACT`, `DERIVED_ANALYTIC`, or `HUMAN_DECISION`.
4. **Audit Hash:** Cryptographic digest of the prompt, model version, and completion stored in `enterprise_audit_log`.

---

## 3. Investigation & Checkpoint Workflow

```text
1. Anomaly Flagged by Analytics
   └── Event: anomaly.detected (e.g. 40% sudden drop in vaccination submissions in District X)

2. AI Investigation Spawned
   ├── Aggregates historical district submissions
   ├── Correlates with weather / road GIS layers from StatSpatial
   └── Formulates root-cause hypothesis (e.g. Road flooded in sub-county Y)

3. Human Officer Review
   ├── Officer inspects attached evidence in Command Centre
   ├── Opens StatChat thread with District Field Supervisor
   └── Records official Decision Record (e.g. Deploy mobile boat team)

4. Workflow Execution & Outcome Capture
   └── Action item tracked to completion and indexed into Knowledge Graph
```
